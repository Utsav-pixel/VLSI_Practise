Your Starting Point

4+ years in EC → IT → switching back to VLSI. Strong in logic gates & digital electronics — weak on transistor-level CMOS/MOSFET physics. This course refreshes fundamentals first (Phase 1), then builds up through RTL design, physical design, fabrication, advanced topics, real projects, and finally an NPTEL certification + job-prep sprint.

Course Map

PHASE 1 · 6–8 weeks

Electronics & CMOS Refresh

Semiconductor physics, pn junction, MOSFET regions & short-channel effects, CMOS inverter, CMOS logic gate design. Your priority start.
PHASE 2 · 6–8 weeks

Memory, Sequential & Arithmetic Circuits

SRAM/DRAM cells, latches & flip-flops, adders & multipliers, interconnect and signal integrity.
PHASE 3 · 8–10 weeks

RTL Design & Verification

Verilog/SystemVerilog from scratch, testbenches, coverage, and logic synthesis (RTL → gates). Highest industry priority.
PHASE 4 · 8 weeks

Physical Design & STA

Floorplanning, placement, clock tree synthesis, routing, and static timing analysis — the core "chip design" skillset.
PHASE 5 · 4–5 weeks

Semiconductor Fabrication

Crystal growth, oxidation, lithography, doping, deposition, etching, and the full CMOS process flow. NPTEL-heavy.
PHASE 6 · 8 weeks

DFT, Low Power & Custom Layout

Scan/ATPG/BIST/JTAG, low-power design techniques, custom layout rules with a free open-source PDK, analog basics.
PHASE 7 & 8 · 10–12 weeks

Projects, NPTEL & Job Prep

RISC-V processor project, AES core, GitHub portfolio, NPTEL exam strategy + formula sheet, 50 interview questions, target companies.
